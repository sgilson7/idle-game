# Glossary

The source of truth for what each word in this tool means. Strings, tests, the plan and the README use a term in the sense given here. The page shows a definition when a reader hovers the term.

Write each entry as a `### Term` heading, then one or two plain sentences, then an optional `Not:` line listing words that must not be used for it. A number a rule decides is a `{placeholder}` the core fills, so the glossary cannot disagree with the rules. Keep entries in alphabetical order. `crates/core/tests/copy.rs` checks the format, the placeholders and the `Not:` words; `TONE.md` governs the prose.

### Bank

Where everything you gather and make is kept, with no limit on space. Tap a stack to sell it for gold.

Not: inventory, stash

### Blueprint

A design the Inventor researches with parts and gold over game time. Once done, the workshop can build what it describes.

### Book

Something Lore reads while you hold it, turning notes into insight. The first reading teaches its spell. Each region has a common book, found now and then in its work and fights, and a rare one in its dungeon chests and from its champions. Smithing's masterwork is something else.

### Caravan

A cart-load of one of your goods sent to a town. It comes back on its own, with gold at that town's price and a little of what the town sells.

Not: shipment

### Codex

A tome Lore has researched. The Cult's top ranks each ask for one, and each teaches the Cult a rite.

### Compost

Ashes used when you plant. A crop planted with compost grows faster.

### Cult

The engine that opens late: cultists read the teachings you link on its graph, and cast rites in the background.

Not: religion

### Cultist

A member of your Cult, converted with renown or with gold from your estate. More cultists cast faster and more surely.

### Deed

What a noble rank from Squire on asks besides gold and rooms: defeating that rank's champion, or a tribute of fine goods.

### Dictionary

The "?" beside the flag. Type part of a name to see where an item comes from, what uses it, and a way to the tab for each; skills, engines, enemies and ranks have entries too. It also counts the items you have come across.

Not: encyclopedia

### Dungeon

A run through some of one region's enemies in a fixed order, ending at a champion, with a chest at the end. Each region has three, each opened by clearing the one before. Running out of food starts it over.

### Engine

A long ladder beside the skills that spends what they make and gives some back: combat, the Trader, the estate, expeditions, the Inventor, the sky and the Cult.

### Expedition

A party sent through a site's events, one after another. It ends when the site is cleared, when an offering's time runs out, when everyone is beaten, or when you call the party home.

### Flag

A playtest note: how a moment felt, the pace, and anything you want to say, saved with what you were doing.

### Gold

What the shop pays for items and charges for tools. It never costs real money.

Not: coins, gems

### Husbandry

A skill of raising animals in pens. A young animal placed in a pen gives its produce every so often while you do anything else, eating its feed from your bank each time you collect.

### Insight

What reading a book gives Lore. Decoding a star pattern and researching a tome spend it.

### Invention

A gadget or a machine the workshop builds from a blueprint. Building it again raises its level and makes it faster; switched off, it keeps its level and does nothing.

### Knight

A member hired at the Estate for gold and a wage each hour, sent on expeditions. Knights are stronger than servants and weaker than followers.

### Mastery

A level for one tree, rock, fishing spot or recipe, rising to the same top level as a skill. Its checkpoints make that one action better.

### Order

A town's request for a number of one item, paying gold and Trading XP. Each town posts new ones every few hours.

### Paddock

An estate room for Husbandry. Each level built is one more pen, ready to use without planks.

Not: field, enclosure

### Party

The members you send on an expedition, each with its own gear, food and runes. Its size grows with Exploration.

### Pen

Where Husbandry keeps one animal. Most open at a Husbandry level and are built once from planks; each level of the estate's paddock adds one more. Letting the animal go leaves the pen empty.

Not: stall, cage

### Plan

A short list of actions that run in order, while you watch or while you are away.

Not: queue, script

### Prestige

Starting every skill again from the first level, in exchange for more XP from then on. Regions and engines you had open stay open; their ranks and the estate's rooms start over.

Not: rebirth, ascension

### Purchase

The one payment in the app. It opens the Coast, the Peaks, the Wastes, the Beyond and the Moon, each at its total level; the Vale and the web version are free.

Not: DLC, expansion pack, in-app purchase

### Rank

A step on an engine's ladder. Each rank asks for something and makes the engine better.

### Recipe

A rare drop from an enemy that unlocks one unique food, weapon or armour. Recipes stay through prestige.

### Region

One part of the world: the Vale, the Coast, the Peaks, the Wastes, the Beyond or the Moon. Each opens at a total level and adds new things to every skill and engine; the Beyond also needs the veil open, and the Moon the rocket ship launched.

Not: zone, area

### Relic

A dungeon's own ring or amulet, found only in its chest.

### Rite

A spell the Cult casts when every teaching it needs is linked in one group on the graph: a buff for a while, things from beyond, a hidden recipe, or the veil.

Not: prayer

### Rocket ship

The last thing Crafting builds, in the workshop, from parts made across every skill. Launched once, it opens the Moon for good, and it is kept through prestige.

Not: spaceship, shuttle

### Servant

A worker on your estate who does one of your actions at half speed, alongside you, for food and a wage each hour.

### Shadow clone

A shadow of you that steps out of the veil and runs one of your saved plans alongside you, at a share of your speed. Its forms are bound on the Cult tab with teachings and codexes.

Not: mirror image

### Siege

A town you trade with asking for help against an enemy that assails it. Defeat enough of them and it thanks you with a higher top level for every room or a new building.

### Sign

The part of the sky that is up now. Training a skill under its sign gathers starlight.

### Site

A place to explore, such as an old manor or an ancient crypt: a line of events, deeper ones harder, with finds that turn up nowhere else. Its map turns up in Lore, dungeon chests and runecrafting.

### Sky map

A star pattern Lore has decoded. The Observatory's top ranks spend them.

### Spell

A spell Lore has learned from a book. Readied, it is cast with every swing in a fight while you hold its runes, adding to your max hit beside your weapon. Each spell has its own level, from the XP of its hits that land, and adds more at each level.

### Star pattern

A pattern the Observatory writes down now and then while it gathers starlight, for Lore to decode into a sky map.

### Starlight

What training a skill under its sign gathers. Attunement spends it.

### Statistics

The screen under More of what you have done in all your time playing: gold earned, enemies defeated, things gathered and made, and each skill's count and time. Prestige keeps it.

### Teaching

A scroll the Cult reads, written in Runecrafting or found in a dungeon's chest.

### Tome

An old book of ancient evil the Cult's rites turn up, for Lore to research into a codex.

### Total level

All your skill levels added together. It opens the engines and prestige.

### Veil

What stands between the world and the Beyond. A rite opens it once the Cult and the Observatory are both high enough, and it stays open through prestige.

Not: portal

### Warmth

What burning logs gives the house for a while: Smithing, Cooking and servants work a little faster. Better wood warms more, and for longer.

### XP

Experience. Each action gives XP to its skill; enough XP raises the skill's level.
