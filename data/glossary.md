# Glossary

The source of truth for what each word in this tool means. Strings, tests, the plan and the README use a term in the sense given here. The page shows a definition when a reader hovers the term.

Write each entry as a `### Term` heading, then one or two plain sentences, then an optional `Not:` line listing words that must not be used for it. A number a rule decides is a `{placeholder}` the core fills, so the glossary cannot disagree with the rules. Keep entries in alphabetical order. `crates/core/tests/copy.rs` checks the format, the placeholders and the `Not:` words; `TONE.md` governs the prose.

### Agent

A computer on the Moon base's network, made by Crafting and dropped anywhere on the base. An agent that reaches the dish either listens, catching its ground's kind of signal, or tunes onto one action and gives it its ground's effect for nothing.

### Augment

An item that changes one rule of a solo trial while you hold it and have it switched on, two at a time: made in Invention.

### Bank

Where everything you gather and make is kept, with no limit on space. Tap a stack to sell it for gold.

Not: inventory, stash

### Blueprint

A design the Inventor researches with parts and gold over game time. Once done, the workshop can build what it describes.

### Book

Something Lore reads while you hold it, turning notes into insight. The first reading teaches its spell. Each region has a common book, found now and then in its work and fights, and a rare one in its dungeon chests and from its champions. Smithing's masterwork is something else.

### Calibration

What the telescope does to a faint light with the star fixes it takes: once a planet has taken all it needs, it is calibrated, and stays so through prestige.

### Caravan

A cart-load of one of your goods sent to a town. It comes back on its own, with gold at that town's price and a little of what the town sells.

Not: shipment

### Chanting

What a cultist does who is set to neither a rite nor a study: it casts nothing, but shortens every cycle more than a cultist performing does.

### Chemistry

Prismatic Valley's own skill: experiments at the bench that mix two reagents in proportion to discover compounds.

### Codex

A tome Lore has researched. The Cult's top ranks each ask for one, and each teaches the Cult a rite.

### Compost

Ashes used when you plant. A crop planted with compost grows faster.

### Compound

What Chemistry makes when the right two reagents are mixed in the right proportions.

### Computerization

The skill of the Moon base's network: the dish, agents and switches dropped where you choose, and cables between them. Its actions refine each kind of signal into its kind of wave.

### Crew

The cultists set to perform one rite. A rite is cast only with its full crew; cultists not in a crew may research, or wait idle.

### Cult

The engine that opens late: cultists read the teachings you link on its graph, and cast rites in the background.

Not: religion

### Cultist

A member of your Cult, converted with renown or with gold from your estate. Each performs a rite in a crew, researches new rites, chants to shorten the cycle, or waits idle. More cultists performing cast faster and more surely.

### Data center

A piece on the Moon base that, cabled to the dish, runs one job a minute (decoding signals into lunar data, or making star fixes) and runs models.

### Deed

What a noble rank from Squire on asks besides gold and rooms: defeating that rank's champion, or a tribute of fine goods.

### Devotion

The Cult's own level, grown steadily while its rites run and its researchers study, and by a great deal when a study finds its rite. It makes rites land more often, and each of the Cult's ranks asks for some.

### Dictionary

The "?" beside the flag. Type part of a name to see where an item comes from, what uses it, and a way to the tab for each; skills, engines, enemies and ranks have entries too. It also counts the items you have come across.

Not: encyclopedia

### Dungeon

A run through some of one region's enemies in a fixed order, ending at a champion, with a chest at the end. Each region has three, each opened by clearing the one before. Running out of food starts it over.

### Effects

The top bar's button, and its sheet, of everything that changes your numbers now: each strength running out, in full, then what works while you have it (prestige, jewellery, armour, weapons, gadgets, tuning rods and rank perks). Tapping a strength's chip opens it too.

Not: passives

### Engine

A long ladder beside the skills that spends what they make and gives some back: combat, the Trader, the estate, expeditions, the Inventor, the sky and the Cult.

### Expedition

A party sent through a site's events, one after another. It ends when the site is cleared, when an offering's time runs out, when everyone is beaten, or when you call the party home.

### Flag

A playtest note: how a moment felt, the pace, and anything you want to say, saved with what you were doing.

### Formulary

Chemistry's list of the compounds you have discovered, how each is mixed, and the clues the four lands' chests and books have given.

### Gold

What the shop pays for items and charges for tools. It never costs real money.

Not: coins, gems

### Ground

The Moon base's three kinds of land: the crater rim, the mare and the ridge. The ground under an agent decides its signals and what it gives an action it tunes onto; the dish's ground adds a signal of its kind for each listening agent.

### Husbandry

A skill of raising animals in pens. A young animal placed in a pen gives its produce every so often while you do anything else, eating its feed from your bank each time you collect.

### Insight

What reading a book gives Lore. Decoding a star pattern and researching a tome spend it.

### Invention

A gadget or a machine the workshop builds from a blueprint. Building it again raises its level and makes it faster; switched off, it keeps its level and does nothing.

### Keep booking

A switch on one venue at a time on the Stage. While it is on, the venue is booked again each time its show ends, as soon as the crowd's food is in the bank.

### Knight

One who goes on expeditions: a squire hired at the Estate, or one of thirteen knights each found its own way. A knight levels up from the expeditions it comes home from and gets a point each level for its tree; its weapon and armour are its own until changed.

### Knight's order

A standing order in the last town of the Vale, the Coast or the Peaks: three orders filled one after another, open from the moment its land opens. The third brings that land's knight, and the order is gone.

### Know-how

What a knight's tree teaches it of some skills: in a check, it counts as work done in them.

### Lunar datum

What the data center decodes from signals. Lunar data make star fixes, run models, and speed the Moon's best bar, cloth and runes.

### Mastery

A level for one tree, rock, fishing spot or recipe, rising to the same top level as a skill. Its checkpoints make that one action better.

### Model

An hour's strength the data center runs for lunar data: the tide model, the orbit model or the deep-sky model.

### Network relay

A piece on the Moon base that copies one listening agent's packets onto two cables onward, each of which must reach the dish, so that agent catches twice the signals.

### Order

A town's request for a number of one item, paying gold and Trading XP. Each town posts new ones every few hours. Gold buys a new set sooner, dearer each time until the town posts its own.

### Paddock

An estate room for Husbandry. Each level built is one more pen, ready to use without planks.

Not: field, enclosure

### Party

The knights you send on an expedition, each with its own gear and the food and runes you give it. Its size grows with Exploration.

### Pen

Where Husbandry keeps one animal. Most open at a Husbandry level and are built once from planks; each level of the estate's paddock adds one more. Letting the animal go leaves the pen empty.

Not: stall, cage

### Petting

Tapping an animal in one of your pens for Husbandry XP, as much each time as placing it gives. It happens only while you tap.

### Plan

A short list of actions that run in order, while you watch or while you are away.

Not: queue, script

### Planet

A faint light the telescope can calibrate: Cinder, Pelagos, Thren and Nyx.

### Prismatic map

The map to the Prismatic Gate: a quarter from each starting land's deepest site, joined by Lore.

### Prestige

Starting every skill again from the first level, in exchange for more XP from then on. Regions and engines you had open stay open; their ranks and the estate's rooms start over. Servants stay, with as many places as you had.

Not: rebirth, ascension

### Purchase

The one payment in the app. It opens the Coast, the Peaks, the Wastes, the Beyond and the Moon, each at its total level; the Vale and the web version are free.

Not: DLC, expansion pack, in-app purchase

### Rank

A step on an engine's ladder. Each rank asks for something and makes the engine better.

### Reagent

What goes in the flask at Chemistry's bench: charcoal, sea salt, quicksilver, brimstone, or a compound already made.

### Recipe

A rare drop from an enemy that unlocks one unique food, weapon or armour. Recipes stay through prestige.

### Region

One part of the world: the Vale, the Coast, the Peaks, the Wastes, the Beyond, the Moon or Prismatic Valley. Each opens at a total level and adds new things to every skill and engine; the Beyond also needs the veil open, the Moon the rocket ship launched, and Prismatic Valley the Prismatic Gate cleared.

Not: zone, area

### Relic

A dungeon's own ring or amulet, found only in its chest. One lost to a prestige comes back from that dungeon's next chest.

### Revive

Ending a resting knight's rest at once, for gold. The price doubles with each revive, and comes back down a day after the first.

### Rite

A spell the Cult casts when every teaching it needs is linked in one group on the graph: a buff for a while, things from beyond, a hidden recipe, or the veil.

Not: prayer

### Rocket ship

The last thing Crafting builds, in the workshop, from parts made across every skill. Launched once, it opens the Moon for good, and it is kept through prestige.

Not: spaceship, shuttle

### Servant

A worker on your estate who does one of your actions at half speed, alongside you, for food and a wage each hour.

### Shadow clone

A shadow of you that steps out of the veil and runs one of your saved plans alongside you, at a share of your speed. Its forms are bound on the Cult tab with teachings and codexes.

Not: mirror image

### Siege

A town you trade with asking for help against an enemy that assails it. Defeat enough of them and it thanks you with a higher top level for every room or a new building.

### Sign

The part of the sky that is up now. Training a skill under its sign gathers starlight.

### Signal

What a listening agent on the Moon base catches from the cosmos each minute while its ping gets through: sharp on the crater rim, deep on the mare, bright on the ridge. Computerization refines each into its kind of wave.

### Site

A place to explore, such as an old manor or an ancient crypt: a line of events, deeper ones harder, with finds that turn up nowhere else. Its map turns up in Lore, dungeon chests and runecrafting.

### Sky map

A star pattern Lore has decoded. The Observatory's top ranks spend them.

### Spell

A spell Lore has learned from a book. Readied, it is cast with every swing in a fight while you hold its runes, adding to your max hit beside your weapon. Each spell has its own level, from the XP of its hits that land, and adds more at each level.

### Squire

A knight hired at the Estate for gold and a wage each hour, with a small tree of its own.

### Star fix

What the data center makes from lunar data and bright signals. The telescope takes star fixes to calibrate a planet.

### Star pattern

A pattern the Observatory writes down now and then while it gathers starlight, for Lore to decode into a sky map.

### Starlight

What training a skill under its sign gathers. Attunement spends it.

### Statistics

The screen under More of what you have done in all your time playing: gold earned, enemies defeated, things gathered and made, and each skill's count and time. Prestige keeps it.

### Teaching

A scroll the Cult reads, written in Runecrafting or found in a dungeon's chest.

### Telescope

A piece on the Moon base, only on the ridge, that takes star fixes toward calibrating the planet it is aimed at.

### Tome

An old book of ancient evil the Cult's rites turn up, for Lore to research into a codex.

### Total level

All your skill levels added together. It opens the engines and prestige.

### Trial

A solo dungeon in each endgame land, a fourth after its three, with a rule of its own that only some builds and augments get through; health at zero ends its run.

### Tuning rod

A rod made by Attunement and put on one action with its Tune button: a speed rod makes it twice as fast, a yield rod twice as plentiful, an insight rod gives twice the XP. It spends one wave of its kind for each minute of work it tunes.

### Veil

What stands between the world and the Beyond. A rite opens it once the Cult and the Observatory are both high enough, and it stays open through prestige.

Not: portal

### Warmth

What burning logs gives the house for a while: Smithing, Cooking and servants work a little faster. Better wood warms more, and for longer.

### Wave

What Computerization refines from signals: speed waves from sharp signals, yield waves from deep, insight waves from bright. A tuning rod spends one of its kind for each minute of work it tunes.

### XP

Experience. Each action gives XP to its skill; enough XP raises the skill's level.
